Financial Performance Analysis

Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing a company’s financial performance using Microsoft Excel. It examines revenue, operating expenses, profitability, budget performance and expense variances to support better financial decision-making.

Key Financial Findings

* Total Annual Revenue: ₦85,600,000
* Total Annual Expenses: ₦35,320,000
* Annual Operating Profit: ₦50,280,000
* Operating Profit Margin: 58.74%
* Favourable Revenue Budget Variance: ₦1,800,000 (2.15%)
* Unfavourable Expense Budget Variance: ₦1,320,000 (3.88%)

Management Insights

Revenue exceeded the annual budget by 2.15%, indicating positive revenue performance. However, actual expenses exceeded the approved budget by 3.88%.

December recorded the highest expense overspending at ₦250,000. Management should review the causes of the overspending and assess whether the additional expenditure generated sufficient value.

Recommendations

* Maintain revenue growth.
* Strengthen expense monitoring and budgetary control.
* Evaluate the returns generated by marketing and other business expenses.
* Review financial performance regularly to support better business decisions.
